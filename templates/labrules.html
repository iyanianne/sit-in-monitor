Laboratory Rules and Guidelines

UNIVERSITY OF CEBU - MAIN CAMPUS

College of Information and Computer Studies

To avoid embarrassment and maintain camaraderie with your friends and superiors at our laboratories, please observe the following:

{% for i in range(1, 13) %}
{% if i == 1 %}

{{ i }}. Maintain silence, proper decorum, and discipline inside the laboratory. Mobile phones, walkmans and other personal pieces of equipment must be switched off.

{% elif i == 2 %}

{{ i }}. Games are not allowed inside the lab. This includes computer-related games, card games and other games that may disturb the operation of the lab.

{% elif i == 3 %}

{{ i }}. Surfing the Internet is allowed only with the permission of the instructor. Downloading and installing of software are strictly prohibited.

{% elif i == 4 %}

{{ i }}. Getting access to other websites not related to the course (especially pornographic and illicit sites) is strictly prohibited.

{% elif i == 5 %}

{{ i }}. Deleting computer files and changing the set-up of the computer is a major offense.

{% elif i == 6 %}

{{ i }}. Observe computer time usage carefully. A fifteen-minute allowance is given for each use. Otherwise, the unit will be given to those who wish to "sit-in".

{% elif i == 7 %}

{{ i }}. Observe proper decorum while inside the laboratory:

  • Do not get inside the lab unless the instructor is present.
  • All bags, knapsacks, and the likes must be deposited at the counter.
  • Follow the seating arrangement of your instructor.
  • At the end of class, all software programs must be closed.
  • Return all chairs to their proper places after using.
{% elif i == 8 %}

{{ i }}. Chewing gum, eating, drinking, smoking, and other forms of vandalism are prohibited inside the lab.

{% elif i == 9 %}

{{ i }}. Anyone causing a continual disturbance will be asked to leave the lab. Acts or gestures offensive to the members of the community, including public display of physical intimacy, are not tolerated.

{% elif i == 10 %}

{{ i }}. Persons exhibiting hostile or threatening behavior such as yelling, swearing, or disregarding requests made by lab personnel will be asked to leave the lab.

{% elif i == 11 %}

{{ i }}. For serious offense, the lab personnel may call the Civil Security Office (CSU) for assistance.

{% elif i == 12 %}

{{ i }}. Any technical problem or difficulty must be addressed to the laboratory supervisor, student assistant or instructor immediately.

{% endif %}
{% endfor %}

DISCIPLINARY ACTION

First Offense - The Head or the Dean or OIC recommends to the Guidance Center for a suspension from classes for each offender.

Second and Subsequent Offenses - A recommendation for a heavier sanction will be endorsed to the Guidance Center.
