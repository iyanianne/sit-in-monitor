SIT-IN RULES AND REGULATIONS

To maintain order and ensure proper use of laboratory facilities, please observe the following sit-in rules:

1. Only CICS students are allowed to sit-in in the laboratory.

2. Students must present their ID to the laboratory personnel before using the computer.

3. Students are given a maximum of 2 hours to use the computer. Extension may be granted if there are no other students waiting.

4. Students must log their sit-in session in the monitoring system.

5. Students must follow all laboratory rules and regulations while using the facilities.

6. Priority is given to students with academic requirements or research purposes.

7. Students must save their work and log out properly after using the computer.

8. The laboratory personnel has the right to terminate a student's session if they violate any of the rules.


IMPORTANT REMINDERS
  • Keep your belongings with you at all times. The laboratory is not responsible for lost items.
  • Report any technical issues to the laboratory personnel immediately.
  • Be considerate of other students who may need to use the facilities.
  • Clean your workspace before leaving.
